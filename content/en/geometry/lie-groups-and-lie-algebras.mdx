# Matrix Lie Groups SO(3)/SE(3) & Lie Algebras so(3)/se(3)

> "Why do Euler angles suffer from kinematic gimbal lock, why are $3 \times 3$ rotation matrices over-parameterized with 6 non-linear constraints, and how does Lie Group Theory unlock singularity-free geometric optimization in 3D robotics?"

In modern robotics (Visual-Inertial Odometry, Factor Graph SLAM, and Operational Space Manipulation), 3D spatial rotations $SO(3)$ and rigid body poses $SE(3)$ do not form standard Euclidean vector spaces. Instead, they form smooth **Differentiable Manifolds endowed with Group Structure (Lie Groups)**.

---

## 1. The Lie Group $SO(3)$ & Its Tangent Lie Algebra $\mathfrak{so}(3)$

The Special Orthogonal group $SO(3)$ formalizes rigid 3D rotations without scale distortion:

$$
SO(3) = \{ \mathbf{R} \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3} \mid \mathbf{R}^T \mathbf{R} = \mathbf{I}, \, \det(\mathbf{R}) = +1 \}
$$

The tangent space at the identity matrix $\mathbf{I}$ is the **Lie Algebra $\mathfrak{so}(3)$**, populated by $3 \times 3$ skew-symmetric matrices:

$$
[\boldsymbol{\omega}]_\times = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -\omega_3 & \omega_2 \\ \omega_3 & 0 & -\omega_1 \\ -\omega_2 & \omega_1 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \in \mathfrak{so}(3), \quad \boldsymbol{\omega} \in \mathbb{R}^3
$$

The isomorphic *vee* $(\cdot)^\vee$ and *hat* $(\cdot)^\wedge$ operators map between unconstrained rotation vectors $\mathbb{R}^3$ and Lie algebra matrices $\mathfrak{so}(3)$:

$$
\boldsymbol{\omega} = [\boldsymbol{\omega}]_\times^\vee \in \mathbb{R}^3, \quad [\boldsymbol{\omega}]_\times = \boldsymbol{\omega}^\wedge \in \mathfrak{so}(3)
$$

---

## 2. Rodrigues Exponential & Matrix Logarithm Maps

The matrix exponential map wraps straight lines in the tangent space $\mathfrak{so}(3)$ onto closed geodesic rotation arcs on the $SO(3)$ manifold:

$$
\mathbf{R} = \exp([\boldsymbol{\omega}]_\times) = \mathbf{I} + \frac{\sin\theta}{\theta} [\boldsymbol{\omega}]_\times + \frac{1 - \cos\theta}{\theta^2} [\boldsymbol{\omega}]_\times^2, \quad \theta = \|\boldsymbol{\omega}\|
$$

### The Logarithm Map ($\log: SO(3) \to \mathfrak{so}(3)$):
Extracts the rotation angle $\theta$ and axis from a $3 \times 3$ matrix:

$$
\theta = \arccos\left(\frac{\text{tr}(\mathbf{R}) - 1}{2}\right), \quad [\boldsymbol{\omega}]_\times = \frac{\theta}{2\sin\theta}(\mathbf{R} - \mathbf{R}^T)
$$

---

## 3. Special Euclidean Group $SE(3)$ & Spatial Twists $\mathfrak{se}(3)$

The Special Euclidean group $SE(3)$ combines rotation and translation into a homogeneous matrix:

$$
SE(3) = \left\{ \mathbf{T} = \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{R} & \mathbf{t} \\ \mathbf{0}_{1\times 3} & 1 \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{4 \times 4} \;\middle|\; \mathbf{R} \in SO(3), \, \mathbf{t} \in \mathbb{R}^3 \right\}
$$

A 6-dimensional spatial twist vector $\boldsymbol{\xi} = [\boldsymbol{\rho}^T, \boldsymbol{\phi}^T]^T \in \mathbb{R}^6$ maps to Lie algebra $\mathfrak{se}(3)$ via:

$$
[\boldsymbol{\xi}]_\wedge = \begin{bmatrix} [\boldsymbol{\phi}]_\times & \boldsymbol{\rho} \\ \mathbf{0} & 0 \end{bmatrix} \in \mathfrak{se}(3)
$$

### Closed-Form $SE(3)$ Exponential Map:

$$
\exp([\boldsymbol{\xi}]_\wedge) = \begin{bmatrix} \exp([\boldsymbol{\phi}]_\times) & \mathbf{J}_l(\boldsymbol{\phi}) \boldsymbol{\rho} \\ \mathbf{0} & 1 \end{bmatrix}
$$

Where $\mathbf{J}_l(\boldsymbol{\phi}) = \frac{\sin\theta}{\theta}\mathbf{I} + \left(1 - \frac{\sin\theta}{\theta}\right)\mathbf{n}\mathbf{n}^T + \frac{1-\cos\theta}{\theta}[\mathbf{n}]_\times$ is the $SO(3)$ Left Jacobian.

---

## 4. The Adjoint Representation ($\operatorname{Ad}_{\mathbf{T}}$)

To transform spatial twist velocities or $6 \times 6$ covariance matrices between coordinate frames:

$$
\operatorname{Ad}_{\mathbf{T}} = \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{R} & [\mathbf{t}]_\times \mathbf{R} \\ \mathbf{0}_{3\times 3} & \mathbf{R} \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{6 \times 6}
$$

---

## 5. Manifold Perturbation Calculus: $\boxplus$ and $\boxminus$ Operators

Because standard matrix addition $\mathbf{R}_1 + \mathbf{R}_2 \notin SO(3)$ violates orthogonality, non-linear least squares optimizers (such as Gauss-Newton in GTSAM and Ceres) formulate updates on the tangent space:

$$
\mathbf{T} \boxplus \boldsymbol{\delta} = \mathbf{T} \exp([\boldsymbol{\delta}]_\wedge) \in SE(3)
$$

$$
\mathbf{T}_1 \boxminus \mathbf{T}_2 = \log(\mathbf{T}_2^{-1} \mathbf{T}_1)^\vee \in \mathbb{R}^6
$$
