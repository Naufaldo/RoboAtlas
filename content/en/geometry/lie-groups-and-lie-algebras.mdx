# Matrix Lie Groups SO(3)/SE(3) & Lie Algebras so(3)/se(3)

> "Why do Euler angles suffer from catastrophic gimbal lock, while $3 \times 3$ rotation matrices carry 9 parameters burdened by 6 non-linear orthonormality constraints—and how does Lie Group theory enable singularity-free spatial optimization?"

In modern robotics (Visual-Inertial Odometry, Factor Graph SLAM, and Manipulator Control), 3D spatial rotations $SO(3)$ and rigid body transformations $SE(3)$ do not form standard Euclidean vector spaces under addition. Instead, they constitute smooth **Differentiable Manifolds (Lie Groups)**.

---

## 1. The Lie Group $SO(3)$ & Its Tangent Lie Algebra $\mathfrak{so}(3)$

The Special Orthogonal Group $SO(3)$ is defined as:

$$
SO(3) = \{ \mathbf{R} \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3} \mid \mathbf{R}^T \mathbf{R} = \mathbf{I}, \, \det(\mathbf{R}) = +1 \}
$$

The tangent space attached to the identity element $\mathbf{I}$ is the **Lie Algebra $\mathfrak{so}(3)$**, comprising $3 \times 3$ skew-symmetric matrices parameterized by twist vector $\boldsymbol{\omega} \in \mathbb{R}^3$:

$$
[\boldsymbol{\omega}]_\times = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -\omega_3 & \omega_2 \\ \omega_3 & 0 & -\omega_1 \\ -\omega_2 & \omega_1 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \in \mathfrak{so}(3)
$$

---

## 2. The Exponential Map & Rodrigues' Formula

The Exponential Map ($\exp: \mathfrak{so}(3) \to SO(3)$) seamlessly projects tangent vectors onto the curved manifold:

$$
\mathbf{R} = \exp([\boldsymbol{\omega}]_\times) = \mathbf{I} + \frac{\sin\theta}{\theta} [\boldsymbol{\omega}]_\times + \frac{1 - \cos\theta}{\theta^2} [\boldsymbol{\omega}]_\times^2, \quad \theta = \|\boldsymbol{\omega}\|
$$

### The Matrix Logarithm ($\log: SO(3) \to \mathfrak{so}(3)$):

$$
\theta = \arccos\left(\frac{\text{tr}(\mathbf{R}) - 1}{2}\right), \quad [\boldsymbol{\omega}]_\times = \frac{\theta}{2\sin\theta}(\mathbf{R} - \mathbf{R}^T)
$$

---

## 3. Manifold Perturbations & The Plus/Minus Operators ($\boxplus / \boxminus$)

Because naive matrix addition $\mathbf{R}_1 + \mathbf{R}_2 \notin SO(3)$, manifold optimization updates are defined via geodesic retraction:

$$
\mathbf{R} \boxplus \boldsymbol{\delta} = \mathbf{R} \exp([\boldsymbol{\delta}]_\times) \in SO(3)
$$

$$
\mathbf{R}_1 \boxminus \mathbf{R}_2 = \log(\mathbf{R}_2^T \mathbf{R}_1)^\vee \in \mathbb{R}^3
$$
