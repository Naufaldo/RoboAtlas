# Information-Theoretic Autonomous Exploration & Local-Global Strategy (LAGS)

> "Why do purely greedy information-gain robotic exploration algorithms frequently leave behind unmapped pockets in map corners, causing catastrophic trajectory thrashing and backtracking in the final phase of autonomous mapping missions?"

In urban search and rescue (USAR), subterranean mine prospecting, and autonomous domestic cleaning, a mobile robot must explore and map an unknown spatial environment as rapidly and efficiently as possible without a prior blueprint.

According to *MDPI Sensors 2023 (Feng et al.)*, traditional frontier-based approaches suffer from the **Regional Legacy Problem** (isolated unexplored pockets left behind by myopic local greed). The **Local-and-Global Strategy (LAGS)** framework synthesizes Shannon information theory, Gaussian Process Regression (GPR), *Bayesian Optimization (BO)*, and *Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL)* to achieve time-optimal spatial coverage.

---

## 1. Shannon Entropy of Occupancy Grids & Mutual Information (MI)

Spatial map uncertainty over occupancy grid $\mathbf{m} = \{m_i\}$ is quantified via **Shannon Information Entropy**:

$$
H(\mathbf{m}) = \sum_{i} H(m_i) = - \sum_{i} \left[ p(m_i) \log_2 p(m_i) + (1 - p(m_i)) \log_2 (1 - p(m_i)) \right]
$$

- An unexplored cell has prior $p(m_i) = 0.5 \implies H(m_i) = 1.0\text{ bit}$ (maximum entropy).
- A definitively free ($p(m_i) \to 0$) or occupied ($p(m_i) \to 1$) cell has $H(m_i) \to 0\text{ bit}$.

### Mutual Information (MI) Gain Formulation:
The expected entropy reduction when commanding the robot to target pose $\mathbf{x}_k$ and taking range measurement $\mathbf{z}$:

$$
I(\mathbf{m}; \mathbf{z} \mid \mathbf{x}_k) = H(\mathbf{m}) - H(\mathbf{m} \mid \mathbf{z}, \mathbf{x}_k) = \sum_{\text{ray } r} I(m_r; z_r \mid \mathbf{x}_k)
$$

## 2. MI Surface Prediction via Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) & Matérn Kernel

Evaluating analytical raycasting MI at every map pixel is computationally prohibitive ($\mathcal{O}(N^2)$). LAGS accelerates this via:
1. Evaluating exact raycast MI at sparse sample locations.
2. Fitting a **Gaussian Process Regression (GPR)** model with a **Matérn-3/2 Kernel** to infer continuous predictive mean $\mu_{\text{GPR}}(\mathbf{x})$ and variance $\sigma^2_{\text{GPR}}(\mathbf{x})$:

$$
k_{\text{Matérn-3/2}}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{x}') = \sigma_f^2 \left( 1 + \frac{\sqrt{3} \|\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{x}'\|}{\ell} \right) \exp\left( -\frac{\sqrt{3} \|\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{x}'\|}{\ell} \right)
$$

*(The Matérn kernel outperforms standard Squared Exponential RBF by accommodating sharp spatial gradient discontinuities across occluding wall boundaries).*

3. **Bayesian Optimization (GP-UCB)** selects the best local candidate:

$$
\mathbf{x}_{\text{local}}^* = \arg\max_{\mathbf{x} \in \mathcal{C}_{\text{free}}} \left[ \mu_{\text{GPR}}(\mathbf{x}) + \kappa \sigma_{\text{GPR}}(\mathbf{x}) \right]
$$
