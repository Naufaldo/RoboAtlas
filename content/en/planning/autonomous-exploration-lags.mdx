# Information-Theoretic Autonomous Exploration & Local-Global Strategy (LAGS)

> "Why do purely greedy information-theoretic exploration algorithms frequently abandon small unexplored pockets in room corners, causing late-stage exploration efficiency to plummet catastrophically?"

In search-and-rescue, robotic vacuum cleaning, and planetary reconnaissance, autonomous vehicles must construct a complete environment map in minimal time without any prior blueprints. Grounded in research from *MDPI Sensors 2023 (Feng et al.)*, conventional exploration algorithms suffer from severe **Regional Legacy Issues** (small dead-end areas left behind by myopic local greed).

The **Local-and-Global Strategy (LAGS)** algorithm unites Shannon information entropy, Gaussian Process Regression (GPR), Bayesian Optimization (BO), and Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) to achieve high-efficiency exploration while systematically eliminating legacy voids.

---

## 1. Occupancy Map Shannon Entropy & Mutual Information (MI) Gain

Global map uncertainty across an occupancy grid $m$ is quantified via **Shannon Information Entropy**:

$$
H(m) = - \sum_{i} \sum_{j} p(m_{i,j}) \log p(m_{i,j})
$$

Where unobserved cells hold prior probability $p(m_{i,j}) = 0.5$ (each contributing 1 unit of maximal entropy).

### Mutual Information (MI) Reward Gain:
The expected entropy reduction obtained by dispatching the robot to candidate action point $x_{t+1}$:

$$
I(m_t, x_{t+1}) = H(m_t) - H(m_t \mid x_{t+1})
$$

---

## 2. Fast Continuous MI Surface Estimation via Matérn GPR & Bayesian Optimization

Computing analytical raycasting MI over millions of grid pixels is computationally prohibitive ($O(N^2)$). LAGS overcomes this through:
1. **Gaussian Process Regression (GPR)** utilizing the **Matérn Kernel** to interpolate the continuous MI reward surface $I(m, x_*)$ and predicted variance $\sigma^2(x_*)$:
   $$k(x, x') = \frac{2^{1-v}}{\Gamma(v)} \left(\frac{\sqrt{2v}|x-x'|}{\ell}\right)^v K_v\left(\frac{\sqrt{2v}|x-x'|}{\ell}\right)$$
   *(The Matérn kernel captures abrupt step changes in information across obstacle occlusions far better than standard RBF kernels).*
2. **Bayesian Optimization (GP-UCB)** active acquisition sampling:
   $$x_{\text{sample}}^* = \arg\max_{x \in \mathcal{C}_{\text{action}}} \left[ \mu(x) + \kappa \sigma(x) \right]$$

## 3. Dual-Tier LAGS Architecture: Balancing Local Speed and Global Completeness

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Strategy Layer                         │ Operational Behavior & Objective Formulation                           │
├────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Local Exploration Strategy          │ Rapidly uncovers contiguous high-gain frontiers in the local patch:    │
│                                        │ score_local = I(m_local, x) / I_max                                    │
│ 2. Global Perception Strategy (DRL)    │ Identifies nearby small legacy pockets to prevent late backtracking:   │
│                                        │ score_global = w*g + (1-w)*(1-d)                                       │
│ 3. Superimposed Final Decision         │ score_finish = score_local + eta * score_global                         │
└────────────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
