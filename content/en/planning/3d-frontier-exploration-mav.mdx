# 3D Frontier Exploration & MAV Safe Flight Corridors (MDPI 2020)

> "How can an agile Micro Aerial Vehicle (MAV) equipped with lightweight RGB-D depth sensors autonomously explore and map complex 3D collapsed buildings without crashing into obstacles under strict battery and computational constraints?"

Autonomous aerial exploration using **Micro Aerial Vehicles (MAVs)** in disaster-response and subterranean mine inspection demands rapid 3D volumetric mapping under severe payload and on-board compute limitations.

Synthesizing the *MDPI Sensors 2020 (Lu, Redondo, & Campoy)* framework, modern 3D autonomous exploration pipelines integrate:
1. **OctoMap 3D** Probabilistic Volumetric Octree Mapping
2. Cylindrical Candidate Viewpoint Generation & **$k$-Means++** Frontier Clustering
3. **Safe Flight Corridor (SFC)** Constrained Continuous Trajectory Generation

---

## 1. OctoMap 3D Voxel Mapping & RGB-D Sensor Geometry

3D spatial volume is hierarchically indexed using an Octree data structure. Each leaf voxel $n$ stores a log-odds occupancy probability:

$$
L(n \mid z_{1:t}) = L(n \mid z_{1:t-1}) + L(n \mid z_t)
$$

Volumetric spatial uncertainty is quantified via **Shannon Voxel Entropy**:

$$
e(x) = - p(x) \log_2 p(x) - (1 - p(x)) \log_2 (1 - p(x))
$$

### Volumetric Information Gain ($G_v$):
The cumulative entropy encompassed by the frustum cone $R_v$ of the RGB-D sensor (horizontal FOV $\alpha_h$, vertical FOV $\alpha_v$, and range $[d_{\min}, d_{\max}]$):

$$
G_v = \sum_{r \in R_v} \sum_{x \in \text{Voxel}(r)} e(x)
$$

---

## 2. Cylindrical Viewpoint Generation & $k$-Means++ Clustering

A 3D frontier voxel is defined as a free cell adjacent to at least one unknown voxel. For each frontier cluster centroid $(X_f, Y_f, Z_f)$, candidate sensor viewpoints $(X_c, Y_c, Z_c, \theta_c)$ are sampled along a cylindrical shell:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} X_c \\ Y_c \\ Z_c \\ \theta_c \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} X_f + R_i \cos(\theta_i) \\ Y_f + R_i \sin(\theta_i) \\ Z_f \\ \theta_i + \pi \end{bmatrix}
$$

The yaw angle $\theta_c = \theta_i + \pi$ directs the RGB-D camera optical axis directly toward the unmapped frontier volume. **$k$-Means++** clustering aggregates thousands of raw frontier points into $k$ optimal candidate viewpoints ranked by information gain $G_v$.

## 3. Safe Flight Corridors (SFC) & Convex Trajectory Optimization

Once a 3D $A^*$ graph search discovers path waypoints, high-speed flight trajectories are generated using Safe Flight Corridors:

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                          SAFE FLIGHT CORRIDOR (SFC) PIPELINE                            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                             │
                                             ▼
                        ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                        │ 3D A* Path Waypoints: [p_0, p_1, ...]  │
                        └────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                             │
                                             ▼
                        ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                        │ Convex Box Inflation (AABB Expansion)  │
                        │ Expand 6 directions to obstacle limits │
                        │ S_k = [x_min, x_max] x [y_min, y_max]  │
                        └────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                             │
                                             ▼
                        ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                        │ B-Spline / Minimum-Snap Trajectory QP  │
                        │ Strictly bounded inside corridors:     │
                        │ p(t) in S_k intersect S_{k+1}          │
                        └────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The Safe Flight Corridor converts non-linear obstacle avoidance into linear convex bounding constraints (Convex Quadratic Program), guaranteeing collision-free, minimum-jerk flight trajectories solvable in real-time.
