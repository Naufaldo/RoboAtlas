# 3D Frontier-Based Autonomous Exploration & Safe Flight Corridors

> "How can an agile Micro Aerial Vehicle (MAV) equipped with lightweight RGB-D depth sensors map complex, unconstructed 3D disaster ruins without colliding into unseen obstacles?"

Autonomous 3D exploration using Micro Aerial Vehicles (MAVs / Quadrotors) in collapsed subterranean structures or post-disaster search-and-rescue demands volumetric spatial reasoning within strictly constrained on-board computing limits. Grounded in research from *MDPI Sensors 2020 (Lu, Redondo, & Campoy)*, the autonomous aerial exploration framework unifies:
1. Volumetric **3D OctoMap** mapping
2. Polar Candidate Generation & **$k$-Means++** Frontier Clustering
3. Collision-Free **Safe Flight Corridor (SFC)** Inflation Planning

---

## 1. 3D OctoMap Voxel Grid Representation & Volumetric Information Gain

The three-dimensional environment is discretized into hierarchical octree voxels. For each voxel $x$ with occupancy probability $p(x) \in [0, 1]$, spatial uncertainty is quantified by **Voxel Shannon Entropy**:

$$
e(x) = - p(x) \log p(x) - (1 - p(x)) \log(1 - p(x))
$$

### Volumetric Information Gain ($G_v$) for Viewpoint $v$:
The aggregate entropy of all unmapped voxels intersected by the RGB-D camera ray bundle $R_v$:

$$
G_v = \sum_{r \in R_v} \sum_{x \in X} e(x)
$$

---

## 2. Polar Frontier Generation & $k$-Means++ Candidate Filtering

Frontier points along boundaries between free and unknown space are parameterized in cylindrical coordinates around frontier origin $(X_f, Y_f, Z_f)$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} X_c \\ Y_c \\ Z_c \\ \theta_c \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} X_f + R_i \cos(\theta_i) \\ Y_f + R_i \sin(\theta_i) \\ Z_f \\ \theta_i \end{bmatrix}
$$

To eliminate redundant computation from thousands of raw point candidates, **$k$-Means++** clusters the frontier space into $k$ optimal centroid viewpoints ranked by maximal Information Gain $G_v$.

---

## 3. Collision-Free Flight: Safe Flight Corridor (SFC) Generation

Once the optimal target frontier is selected by the global $A^*$ planner, the flight path is protected against obstacle proximity:
1. **Convex Box Inflation**: Each waypoint on the $A^*$ path is inflated along all 6 axis directions until it touches the nearest obstacle voxel boundary.
2. **Center-Corridor Replanning**: The trajectory is smoothed through the geometric centers of the inflated safe boxes, maximizing clearance from walls and narrow doorways.
