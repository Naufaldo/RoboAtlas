# Quadrotor Attitude Dynamics, Cascaded PID Control & Trajectory Tracking

> "How does a multirotor drone calculate real-time motor speeds to execute precise inverted flips, track 3D geometric trajectories at 20 m/s, and recover instantly from violent wind turbulence?"

Autonomous multirotors are **underactuated mechanical systems**: they have 6 spatial degrees of freedom $(x, y, z, \phi, \theta, \psi)$ but only 4 independent scalar rotor thrust inputs $(u_1, u_2, u_3, u_4)$. Translating horizontally in $X$ or $Y$ requires first pitching or rolling the body frame, coupling rotational attitude dynamics directly with translational trajectory acceleration.

## 1. The 6-DOF Quadrotor Equations of Motion

Let $\{W\}$ denote the inertial world frame and $\{B\}$ denote the body frame attached to the drone's center of mass. The full rigid-body flight equations of motion on $SE(3)$ are:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dot{\mathbf{p}} &= \mathbf{v} \\
m \dot{\mathbf{v}} &= m \mathbf{g} + R \, \mathbf{f}_B - D \mathbf{v} \\
\dot{R} &= R \, [\boldsymbol{\omega}]_\times \\
J \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}} &= \boldsymbol{\tau}_B - \boldsymbol{\omega} \times (J \boldsymbol{\omega}) - \boldsymbol{\tau}_{\text{gyro}}
\end{aligned}
$$

Where:
- $m$ is the total vehicle mass (kg) and $J \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3}$ is the body rotational inertia tensor.
- $\mathbf{f}_B = [0, 0, f_{\text{total}}]^T$ is the collective upward aerodynamic thrust along the body $+Z_B$ axis.
- $\boldsymbol{\tau}_B = [\tau_\phi, \tau_\theta, \tau_\psi]^T$ are the aerodynamic roll, pitch, and yaw control torques.
- $\boldsymbol{\tau}_{\text{gyro}} = \sum_{i=1}^4 J_r (\boldsymbol{\omega} \times \mathbf{z}_B) (-1)^i \Omega_i$ is the gyroscopic propeller torque.

## 2. Motor Allocation & Thrust Mixing Matrix

In an 'X' quadrotor configuration where the arm length from CoM to each motor is $d$, motor angular velocities $\Omega_i$ (rad/s) generate net control forces according to:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} f_{\text{total}} \\ \tau_\phi \\ \tau_\theta \\ \tau_\psi \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
k_f & k_f & k_f & k_f \\
-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} d k_f & \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} d k_f & \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} d k_f & -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} d k_f \\
\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} d k_f & \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} d k_f & -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} d k_f & -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} d k_f \\
-k_m & k_m & -k_m & k_m
\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \Omega_1^2 \\ \Omega_2^2 \\ \Omega_3^2 \\ \Omega_4^2 \end{bmatrix}
$$

*(where $k_f$ is the aerodynamic thrust coefficient and $k_m$ is the aerodynamic drag torque coefficient).*

## 3. High-Rate Cascaded PID Control Loop

Modern flight stacks (PX4, ArduPilot, Betaflight) structure autopilot control into a **nested hierarchical loop**:

```text
┌─────────────────────────┐
│ DESIRED POSITION p_des  │
└─────────────────────────┘
            │
            ▼
┌─────────────────────────┐  (50 Hz Outer Position Loop)
│   POSITION PID CONTROL  │ ──► Generates desired velocity v_des
└─────────────────────────┘
            │
            ▼
┌─────────────────────────┐  (100 Hz Velocity Loop)
│   VELOCITY PID CONTROL  │ ──► Generates desired acceleration a_des
└─────────────────────────┘
            │
            ▼
┌─────────────────────────┐  (250 Hz Attitude Loop)
│ ATTITUDE QUATERNION PID │ ──► Computes desired angular rates omega_des
└─────────────────────────┘
            │
            ▼
┌─────────────────────────┐  (1000 Hz Gyro Rate Loop)
│  ANGULAR RATE PID LOOP  │ ──► Computes body torque commands tau
└─────────────────────────┘
            │
            ▼
┌─────────────────────────┐
│ MOTOR MIXING & DSHOT ESC│ ──► Sends PWM / DShot motor signals
└─────────────────────────┘
```

The inner angular rate loop operates at **1 kHz** directly over raw IMU gyroscope measurements, stabilizing rapid attitude disturbances before position tracking error accumulates.

## 4. Minimum Snap Trajectory Generation

Because quadrotors are **differentially flat** with respect to flat outputs $\boldsymbol{\sigma}(t) = [x(t), y(t), z(t), \psi(t)]^T$, smooth trajectories are planned by minimizing the fourth derivative of position (**Snap**):

$$
\min_{\mathbf{p}(t)} \int_0^T \left\| \frac{d^4 \mathbf{p}(t)}{dt^4} \right\|^2 dt = \min_{\mathbf{p}(t)} \int_0^T \| \mathbf{p}^{(4)}(t) \|^2 dt
$$

Minimizing snap minimizes rapid motor acceleration spikes, ensuring actuators do not saturate during aggressive high-speed aerobatic flight.
