# Graph Laplacian Consensus & Multi-Agent Swarm Intelligence

> "How can hundreds of autonomous robots agree on a common direction and velocity without any centralized leader or global supervisor?"

Decentralized multi-agent systems coordinate through local communication graphs $\mathcal{G} = (\mathcal{V}, \mathcal{E})$ where each robot $i$ only exchanges state information with immediate spatial neighbors $\mathcal{N}_i$.

---

## 1. The Graph Laplacian Matrix $\mathcal{L}$

Let communication adjacency matrix $A = [a_{ij}]$ and degree matrix $D = \operatorname{diag}(d_i)$. The **Graph Laplacian** matrix is defined as:

$$
\mathcal{L} = D - A
$$

For an undirected connected graph:
- $\mathcal{L}$ is positive semi-definite ($\mathcal{L} \ge 0$).
- $\mathbf{1} = [1, 1, \dots, 1]^T$ is the eigenvector corresponding to eigenvalue $\lambda_1 = 0$ ($\mathcal{L}\mathbf{1} = \mathbf{0}$).
- The second smallest eigenvalue $\lambda_2(\mathcal{L}) > 0$ is the **Algebraic Connectivity** (Fiedler value), governing the exponential rate of swarm consensus convergence.

---

## 2. Continuous Consensus Protocol

For single-integrator agent dynamics $\dot{\mathbf{x}}_i = \mathbf{u}_i$, the distributed linear consensus control law is:

$$
\mathbf{u}_i = -\sum_{j \in \mathcal{N}_i} a_{ij} (\mathbf{x}_i - \mathbf{x}_j)
$$

In collective matrix form:

$$
\mathbf{\dot{x}} = -\mathcal{L} \mathbf{x}
$$

The state of every agent exponentially converges to the average centroid:

$$
\lim_{t \to \infty} \mathbf{x}_i(t) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=1}^N \mathbf{x}_k(0)
$$
