# Fossen 6-DOF Marine Hydrodynamics, Added Mass & AUV Motion Control

> "Why is navigating an Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) in deep oceanic waters vastly different from flying a drone in air, and how does the Fossen Equations of Motion model hydrodynamic added mass, fluid drag, and restoring buoyancy?"

Operating submerged underwater vehicles (AUVs/ROVs) presents unique robotic challenges: GPS signals cannot penetrate seawater, high water density creates massive **hydrodynamic added mass**, and vehicles must achieve **positive or neutral buoyancy** with stabilizing metacentric restoring moments.

## 1. SNAME 6-DOF Subsea Velocity & Coordinate Vectors

Under the standard **SNAME (Society of Naval Architects and Marine Engineers)** notation:
- Position & Euler Angles in NED World Frame $\{n\}$: $\boldsymbol{\eta} = [x, y, z, \phi, \theta, \psi]^T$
- Linear & Angular Velocities in Body Frame $\{b\}$: $\boldsymbol{\nu} = [u, v, w, p, q, r]^T$ (Surge, Sway, Heave, Roll, Pitch, Yaw)
- Forces & Moments in Body Frame $\{b\}$: $\boldsymbol{\tau} = [X, Y, Z, K, M, N]^T$

```text
┌─────────────────┬──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Degree of Motion│ Body Velocity (nu)   │ Body Force (tau)     │ Physical Robotic Maneuver Description                  │
├─────────────────┼──────────────────────┼──────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Surge        │ u (m/s)              │ X (N)                │ Forward / Backward longitudinal propulsion             │
│ 2. Sway         │ v (m/s)              │ Y (N)                │ Lateral sideways translation (cross-current drift)     │
│ 3. Heave        │ w (m/s)              │ Z (N)                │ Vertical descent / ascent depth regulation             │
│ 4. Roll         │ p (rad/s)            │ K (N·m)              │ Transverse rotational heel (stabilized by buoyancy)    │
│ 5. Pitch        │ q (rad/s)            │ M (N·m)              │ Longitudinal rotational trim (climb / dive angle)      │
│ 6. Yaw          │ r (rad/s)            │ N (N·m)              │ Heading orientation steering                           │
└─────────────────┴──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. The Canonical Fossen 6-DOF Vector Equations of Motion

In 1994, Thor I. Fossen formulated the comprehensive non-linear vector equation governing underwater vehicles:

$$
M \dot{\boldsymbol{\nu}} + C(\boldsymbol{\nu})\boldsymbol{\nu} + D(\boldsymbol{\nu})\boldsymbol{\nu} + \mathbf{g}(\boldsymbol{\eta}) = \boldsymbol{\tau}_{\text{thruster}} + \boldsymbol{\tau}_{\text{env}}
$$

Where the system matrices represent:

### 1. Total System Inertia ($M = M_{\text{RB}} + M_A$):
$$
M = \underbrace{M_{\text{RB}}}_{\text{Rigid-Body Mass (6x6)}} + \underbrace{M_A}_{\text{Hydrodynamic Added Mass (6x6)}}
$$
*Added Mass ($M_A$)* accounts for the mass of surrounding water that must be accelerated whenever the vehicle changes velocity. In water, $M_A$ is often **equal to or greater than the vehicle's dry mass**!

### 2. Total Coriolis & Centripetal Matrix ($C(\boldsymbol{\nu}) = C_{\text{RB}}(\boldsymbol{\nu}) + C_A(\boldsymbol{\nu})$):
Gyroscopic forces arising from vehicle rotation coupled with accelerated fluid.

### 3. Non-Linear Hydrodynamic Damping ($D(\boldsymbol{\nu}) = D_{\text{linear}} + D_{\text{quad}}(\boldsymbol{\nu})$):
$$
D(\boldsymbol{\nu}) = \text{diag}(X_u, Y_v, Z_w, K_p, M_q, N_r) + \text{diag}(X_{u|u}|u|, Y_{v|v}|v|, Z_{w|w}|w|, K_{p|p}|p|, M_{q|q}|q|, N_{r|r}|r|)
$$
Quadratic drag dominates during high-speed swimming maneuvers.

### 4. Restoring Gravitational & Buoyancy Forces ($\mathbf{g}(\boldsymbol{\eta})$):
Let $W = m g$ (Weight) act at Center of Gravity $\mathbf{r}_G = [x_G, y_G, z_G]^T$ and $B = \rho g \nabla$ (Buoyancy) act at Center of Buoyancy $\mathbf{r}_B = [x_B, y_B, z_B]^T$:

$$
\mathbf{g}(\boldsymbol{\eta}) = \begin{bmatrix}
(W - B)\sin\theta \\
-(W - B)\cos\theta\sin\phi \\
-(W - B)\cos\theta\cos\phi \\
-(y_G W - y_B B)\cos\theta\cos\phi + (z_G W - z_B B)\cos\theta\sin\phi \\
(z_G W - z_B B)\sin\theta + (x_G W - x_B B)\cos\theta\cos\phi \\
-(x_G W - x_B B)\cos\theta\sin\phi - (y_G W - y_B B)\sin\theta
\end{bmatrix}
$$

To ensure **passive static stability**, AUVs are engineered with Center of Buoyancy positioned strictly **above** Center of Gravity ($z_B < z_G$).

## 3. Thruster Allocation & Closed-Loop Depth Control

For an AUV equipped with $m$ bidirectional thrusters, the net force vector $\boldsymbol{\tau}$ relates to individual thruster forces $\mathbf{u} = [u_1, \dots, u_m]^T$ via the **Thruster Configuration Matrix $T_{\text{alloc}}$**:

$$
\boldsymbol{\tau} = T_{\text{alloc}} \, \mathbf{u}
$$

Depth control $z(t)$ and pitch trim $\theta(t)$ are achieved via cascaded PID regulation:

$$
u_{\text{depth}} = K_p (z_{\text{target}} - z) + K_i \int (z_{\text{target}} - z) dt + K_d \dot{z}
$$
