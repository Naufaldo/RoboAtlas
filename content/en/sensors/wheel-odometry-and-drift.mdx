# Wheel Odometry, Encoder Dead Reckoning & Systematic Drift

> "Why does an autonomous rover navigating solely by counting wheel rotations inevitably drift away from its true path?"

**Odometry** (from Greek *hodos* meaning "journey" and *metron* meaning "measure") is the process of estimating a robot's incremental change in pose (position and orientation) by integrating optical wheel encoder tick counts over time. This technique is known as **dead reckoning**.

---

## 1. Mathematical Integration of Wheel Rotations

For a differential drive robot with left and right wheel radii $r_L, r_R$ and wheel separation baseline $L$:

When optical encoders measure discrete angular ticks $\Delta N_L, \Delta N_R$ over sampling timestep $\Delta t$:

$$
\Delta s_L = \frac{2\pi r_L}{N_{\text{cpr}}} \Delta N_L, \qquad \Delta s_R = \frac{2\pi r_R}{N_{\text{cpr}}} \Delta N_R
$$

The forward displacement $\Delta s$ and heading rotation $\Delta \theta$ are:

$$
\Delta s = \frac{\Delta s_R + \Delta s_L}{2}, \qquad \Delta \theta = \frac{\Delta s_R - \Delta s_L}{L}
$$

Pose is updated via 2nd-order Runge-Kutta integration:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_{k+1} &= x_k + \Delta s \cos\left(\theta_k + \frac{\Delta \theta}{2}\right) \\
y_{k+1} &= y_k + \Delta s \sin\left(\theta_k + \frac{\Delta \theta}{2}\right) \\
\theta_{k+1} &= \theta_k + \Delta \theta
\end{aligned}
$$

---

## 2. Systematic vs. Stochastic Error Accumulation

Odometry errors fall into two distinct physical classes:

1. **Systematic Errors**: Constant physical imperfections (e.g., left wheel manufactured 1% larger than right wheel, or inaccurate wheelbase $L$). **Systematic errors accumulate quadratically $\mathcal{O}(t^2)$ over travel distance!**
2. **Stochastic (Non-Systematic) Errors**: Random events (wheel slippage on slick floors, carpet irregularities, and encoder quantization noise).

Because orientation errors $\Delta \theta$ rotate the entire subsequent coordinate frame, even a tiny $1^\circ$ heading error causes lateral position error to grow unboundedly over time.
