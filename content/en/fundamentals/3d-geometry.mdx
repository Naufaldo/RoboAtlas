# 3D Spatial Geometry & Rotations

In 3D space, rigid body orientations require 3 rotational degrees of freedom: Roll ($\phi$ around X), Pitch ($\theta$ around Y), and Yaw ($\psi$ around Z).

## $SO(3)$ Rotation Matrix

$$R_{ZYX}(\psi, \theta, \phi) = R_z(\psi) R_y(\theta) R_x(\phi)$$

### Non-Commutative Property
In 3D space, order of rotation matters: $R_x R_y \neq R_y R_x$. Successive matrix multiplication maps coordinate frames through successive rotations.
