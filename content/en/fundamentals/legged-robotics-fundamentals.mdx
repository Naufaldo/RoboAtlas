# Legged Robotics: Zero Moment Point (ZMP), LIPM & Dynamic Locomotion

> "How do bipedal humanoid robots and quadruped robot dogs run dynamically over rough terrain without falling, and how does the Zero Moment Point (ZMP) stability criterion prevent catastrophic rotational tipping?"

Unlike wheeled robots that maintain continuous ground contact, **Legged Robotics** relies on **discrete, hybrid dynamic foot-ground interactions**. Locomotion stability is governed by the **Zero Moment Point (ZMP)** criterion, the **Linear Inverted Pendulum Model (LIPM)**, and the **Capture Point (CP)** framework.

## 2. Mathematical Formulation of Zero Moment Point (ZMP)

Consider a multi-body humanoid robot of total mass $M$ with center of mass $\mathbf{p}_{\text{CoM}} = [x_{\text{CoM}}, y_{\text{CoM}}, z_{\text{CoM}}]^T$.

By D'Alembert's principle, the combined gravitational and inertial moments on the ground plane ($z = 0$) are counteracted by the Ground Reaction Force (GRF). The point $\mathbf{p}_{\text{ZMP}} = [x_{\text{ZMP}}, y_{\text{ZMP}}, 0]^T$ is defined as the point where **horizontal tipping moments vanish**: $\tau_x = 0$ and $\tau_y = 0$:

$$
\tau_x = \sum_i m_i (z_i (\ddot{y}_i + g) - y_i \ddot{z}_i) = 0
$$

$$
\tau_y = \sum_i m_i (x_i \ddot{z}_i - z_i (\ddot{x}_i + g)) = 0
$$

### Closed-Form ZMP Coordinates:

$$
x_{\text{ZMP}} = \frac{\sum_i m_i (\ddot{z}_i + g) x_i - \sum_i m_i z_i \ddot{x}_i - \sum_i I_{yy, i} \dot{\omega}_{y, i}}{\sum_i m_i (\ddot{z}_i + g)}
$$

$$
y_{\text{ZMP}} = \frac{\sum_i m_i (\ddot{z}_i + g) y_i - \sum_i m_i z_i \ddot{y}_i - \sum_i I_{xx, i} \dot{\omega}_{x, i}}{\sum_i m_i (\ddot{z}_i + g)}
$$

## 3. Linear Inverted Pendulum Model (LIPM)

By constraining the robot's center of mass to move on a constant horizontal plane ($z_{\text{CoM}} = z_c \implies \ddot{z} = 0$), non-linear dynamics simplify to a decoupled linear system:

$$
\ddot{x}_{\text{CoM}} = \frac{g}{z_c} (x_{\text{CoM}} - x_{\text{ZMP}}) = \omega_0^2 (x_{\text{CoM}} - x_{\text{ZMP}})
$$

Where the pendulum natural eigenfrequency is:

$$
\omega_0 = \sqrt{\frac{g}{z_c}}
$$

### Analytical Phase-Space Solution:

$$
x(t) = (x_0 - x_{\text{ZMP}}) \cosh(\omega_0 t) + \frac{\dot{x}_0}{\omega_0} \sinh(\omega_0 t) + x_{\text{ZMP}}
$$

## 4. The Capture Point (CP) & Footstep Placement

When external disturbance pushes the ZMP to the boundary of the support polygon, the robot will topple unless it steps to the **Capture Point (CP)** $\boldsymbol{\xi}$:

$$
\boldsymbol{\xi} = \mathbf{x}_{\text{CoM}} + \frac{\mathbf{\dot{x}}_{\text{CoM}}}{\omega_0}
$$

```text
[ Push Disturbance F_push ] ──> CoM Velocity v_CoM ──> Compute Capture Point xi ──> Plant New Footstep at xi (Robot Recovers Equilibrium)
```
