# Introduction to Autonomous Robotics

An autonomous robot is a cyber-physical system capable of independent decision-making in physical environments via sensors and actuators. Unlike simple pre-programmed automation (such as washing machines), robots operate in uncertain, dynamic environments.

## The Sense-Plan-Act Loop

1. **SENSE (Sensors)**:
   - Proprioceptive sensors (internal status: wheel encoders, IMU, battery monitors).
   - Exteroceptive sensors (environment: LiDAR, Stereo Cameras, Ultrasonic Sonar).
2. **PLAN (Computation & Intelligence)**:
   - State Estimation, Spatial Mapping, and Collision-Free Path Planning.
3. **ACT (Actuators & Locomotion)**:
   - DC brushless motors, steering servos, and differential-drive wheel velocity integrators.

## Robot Classifications
- **Fixed Base Manipulators**: Industrial arm robots fixed to factory bases with high repeatable precision.
- **Mobile Terrestrial**: Differential-drive unicycles, Ackermann vehicles, quadruped/humanoid legged walkers.
- **Aerial & Marine**: Multirotor drones and underwater autonomous vehicles (AUVs) operating in 6-DOF spatial configurations.
