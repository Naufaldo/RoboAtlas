# Marine Robotics: Hydrodynamics, Buoyancy & 6-DOF AUV Equations

> "Why are GPS and LiDAR unusable in subsea environments, and how do Autonomous Underwater Vehicles (AUVs) maintain precise depth stability in high-pressure ocean currents?"

**Marine Robotics** encompasses Autonomous Surface Vehicles (USVs) and Autonomous Underwater Vehicles (AUVs/ROVs). Subsea vehicle dynamics are governed by fluid mechanics, viscous hydrodynamic drag, and hydrostatic buoyancy equilibria.

---

## 1. Hydrostatic Equilibrium: Gravitational Weight vs Archimedes Buoyancy

According to Archimedes' principle, the upward hydrostatic buoyancy force $B$ and downward gravitational weight $W$ are:

$$
W = m g, \quad B = \rho_{\text{fluid}} g V_{\text{displaced}}
$$

Where $\rho_{\text{fluid}}$ is seawater density ($\approx 1025 \text{ kg/m}^3$) and $V_{\text{displaced}}$ is the submerged hull volume.

```text
┌─────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Hydrostatic Trim State  │ Net Force Equilibrium                    │ Subsea AUV Behavioral Response                         │
├─────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Neutral Trim (B = W) │ Net vertical force = 0                   │ AUV hovers passively at constant depth without power   │
│ 2. Positive Trim (B > W)│ Net upward buoyancy force                │ Fail-safe mode: AUV floats safely to surface on outage │
│ 3. Negative Trim (B < W)│ Net downward sinking force               │ Rapid descent capability to deep seabed floor          │
└─────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Fossen's 6-DOF Marine Equations of Motion

AUV rigid-body motion across 6 degrees of freedom (*Surge, Sway, Heave, Roll, Pitch, Yaw*) is formulated by Professor Thor I. Fossen:

$$
\mathbf{M} \boldsymbol{\dot{\nu}} + \mathbf{C}(\boldsymbol{\nu})\boldsymbol{\nu} + \mathbf{D}(\boldsymbol{\nu})\boldsymbol{\nu} + \mathbf{g}(\boldsymbol{\eta}) = \boldsymbol{\tau}_{\text{thruster}}
$$

1. **$\mathbf{M} = \mathbf{M}_{RB} + \mathbf{M}_A$**: Total inertia tensor summing rigid-body mass and **Hydrodynamic Added Mass ($\mathbf{M}_A$)** (the effective inertia of the surrounding fluid displaced during acceleration).
2. **$\mathbf{D}(\boldsymbol{\nu})$**: Quadratic hydrodynamic viscous drag damping:
   $$F_{\text{drag}} = \frac{1}{2} \rho C_D A v^2$$
3. **$\mathbf{g}(\boldsymbol{\eta})$**: Restoring hydrostatic forces and moments ensuring the Center of Buoyancy ($CB$) remains positioned above the Center of Gravity ($CG$) for passive metacentric stability.
