# Aerial Robotics: Multirotor Flight Dynamics & 6-DOF Thrust Control

> "How does a quadrotor with only 4 spinning brushless motors maneuver freely across all 6 degrees of freedom without aerodynamic control surfaces like rudders or ailerons?"

**Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs / Quadrotors)** are classic **underactuated dynamic systems**—possessing 6 spatial degrees of freedom (surge, sway, heave, roll, pitch, yaw) governed by only 4 independent motor thrust inputs.

---

## 1. Aerodynamic Thrust & Reaction Torque Mapping

Each propeller spinning at angular rate $\omega_i$ (rad/s) generates aerodynamic lift $f_i$ and drag reaction torque $\tau_{m,i}$:

$$
f_i = k_f \omega_i^2, \quad \tau_{m,i} = k_m \omega_i^2
$$

Where $k_f$ is the aerodynamic thrust coefficient and $k_m$ is the rotor drag torque coefficient.

### Quadrotor Plus-Configuration Actuator Allocation Matrix:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} F_{\text{total}} \\ \tau_\phi \\ \tau_\theta \\ \tau_\psi \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} k_f & k_f & k_f & k_f \\ 0 & -L k_f & 0 & L k_f \\ -L k_f & 0 & L k_f & 0 \\ k_m & -k_m & k_m & -k_m \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \omega_1^2 \\ \omega_2^2 \\ \omega_3^2 \\ \omega_4^2 \end{bmatrix}
$$

```text
┌─────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Flight Maneuver         │ Motor Speed Actuation Scheme             │ Resulting Vehicle Dynamic Response                     │
├─────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Altitude (Throttle)  │ Accelerate all 4 motors identically      │ Total collective thrust F > m*g -> Vertical ascent     │
│ 2. Roll (phi)           │ Accelerate right rotor, decelerate left  │ Differential lateral thrust -> Body tilts along Y-axis │
│ 3. Pitch (theta)        │ Accelerate rear rotor, decelerate front  │ Differential pitch torque -> Body tilts along X-axis   │
│ 4. Yaw (psi)            │ Accelerate CW pair, decelerate CCW pair  │ Net reaction torque imbalance -> Pure heading rotation │
└─────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. 6-DOF Newton-Euler Translational & Rotational Dynamics

Position kinematics and body attitude dynamics are expressed relative to the world inertial frame:

$$
m \mathbf{\ddot{p}} = \mathbf{R}_{B}^{I} \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ F_{\text{total}} \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ m g \end{bmatrix} - \mathbf{F}_{\text{drag}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{I} \boldsymbol{\dot{\omega}}_B + \boldsymbol{\omega}_B \times (\mathbf{I} \boldsymbol{\omega}_B) = \begin{bmatrix} \tau_\phi \\ \tau_\theta \\ \tau_\psi \end{bmatrix}
$$
