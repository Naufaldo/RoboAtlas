# Aerial Robotics: Multirotor Flight Dynamics & 6-DOF Thrust Control

> "How does a quadrotor with only 4 brushless motors maneuver agilely in 6-DOF 3D space without mechanical wings or aerodynamic control surfaces, and why is Differential Flatness the core principle behind modern autonomous drone aerobatics?"

**Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs)** of the multirotor type represent an archetypal **underactuated robotic system**—possessing 6 spatial degrees of freedom $(x, y, z, \phi, \theta, \psi)$ while being actuated by only 4 independent scalar motor thrust commands $(u_1, u_2, u_3, u_4)$.

## 2. Aerodynamic Thrust & Reaction Torque Generation

Each rotor blade spinning at angular velocity $\omega_i$ (rad/s) generates aerodynamic lift $f_i$ and aerodynamic drag torque $\tau_{m,i}$ via Blade Element Momentum Theory:

$$
f_i = k_f \omega_i^2, \quad \tau_{m,i} = k_m \omega_i^2
$$

Where:
- $k_f$ is the aerodynamic thrust coefficient ($\text{N}\cdot\text{s}^2/\text{rad}^2$), governed by air density $\rho$, blade diameter $D$, and thrust coefficient $C_T$.
- $k_m$ is the aerodynamic drag moment coefficient ($\text{N}\cdot\text{m}\cdot\text{s}^2/\text{rad}^2$).

### Mixer Allocation Matrix for X-Configuration:
For a quadrotor with arm length $L$ measured from the Center of Mass ($CM$) to each motor:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} F_{\text{total}} \\ \tau_\phi \\ \tau_\theta \\ \tau_\psi \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} k_f & k_f & k_f & k_f \\ -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} L k_f & -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} L k_f & \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} L k_f & \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} L k_f \\ \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} L k_f & -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} L k_f & -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} L k_f & \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} L k_f \\ k_m & -k_m & k_m & -k_m \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \omega_1^2 \\ \omega_2^2 \\ \omega_3^2 \\ \omega_4^2 \end{bmatrix}
$$

                    \    ^     /

## 3. Mathematical 6-DOF Newton-Euler Equations of Motion

Quadrotor kinematics and dynamics are referenced in two primary coordinate systems:
1. **Inertial World Frame $\{I\}$**: $(X_I, Y_I, Z_I)$ using East-North-Up (ENU) or North-East-Down (NED).
2. **Body-Fixed Frame $\{B\}$**: $(X_B, Y_B, Z_B)$ centered rigidly at the drone's center of mass.

### 1. Translational Position Dynamics (Newton's Second Law):
Translational position $\mathbf{p} = [x, y, z]^T \in \mathbb{R}^3$ satisfies:

$$
m \mathbf{\ddot{p}} = \mathbf{R}_{B}^{I} \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ F_{\text{total}} \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ m g \end{bmatrix} - \mathbf{D}_{\text{drag}} \mathbf{\dot{p}}
$$

Where $\mathbf{R}_B^I \in SO(3)$ is the body-to-world rotation matrix, $m$ is total drone mass, $g = 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$, and $\mathbf{D}_{\text{drag}} = \operatorname{diag}(k_{dx}, k_{dy}, k_{dz})$ models linear aerodynamic translational drag.

### 2. Rotational Attitude Dynamics (Euler's Rigid-Body Equations):
Body angular velocity $\boldsymbol{\omega}_B = [p, q, r]^T \in \mathbb{R}^3$ satisfies:

$$
\mathbf{J} \boldsymbol{\dot{\omega}}_B + \boldsymbol{\omega}_B \times (\mathbf{J} \boldsymbol{\omega}_B) + \boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\text{gyro}} = \begin{bmatrix} \tau_\phi \\ \tau_\theta \\ \tau_\psi \end{bmatrix}
$$

Where:
- $\mathbf{J} = \operatorname{diag}(I_{xx}, I_{yy}, I_{zz})$ is the principal moment of inertia tensor.
- $\boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\text{gyro}} = \sum_{i=1}^4 (-1)^i I_r (\boldsymbol{\omega}_B \times \mathbf{z}_B) \omega_i$ captures rotor gyroscopic precession moments from blade rotational inertia $I_r$.

## 4. Cascaded Flight Control Loop Architecture

To stabilize flight, the control pipeline decouples slow translational position tracking from fast attitude stabilization:

```text
[ Target Waypoint p_des ]
          │
          ▼
┌──────────────────┐   Desired Acceleration  ┌──────────────────┐   Desired Tilt Angles  ┌──────────────────┐   Body Torque tau  ┌──────────────────┐
│  Position Loop   │ ──────────────────────> │  Attitude Loop   │ ─────────────────────> │  Rate Gyro Loop  │ ─────────────────> │  Motor Mixer     │ ──> [ ESC / Motors ]
│  (Outer Loop)    │   a_des = PID(e_p)      │  (Attitude Loop) │   phi_des, theta_des   │  (Inner Loop)    │   tau = PID(e_w)   │  (Matrix Mixer)  │
│  Rate: 50 Hz     │                         │  Rate: 250 Hz    │                        │  Rate: 1000 Hz   │                    │                  │
└──────────────────┘                         └──────────────────┘                        └──────────────────┘                    └──────────────────┘
```

### The Differential Flatness Property
Quadrotors are **differentially flat** with respect to flat outputs $\mathbf{\sigma}(t) = [x(t), y(t), z(t), \psi(t)]^T$. Given any four times continuously differentiable ($C^4$) 3D spatial trajectory with heading, all state variables $(\mathbf{p}, \mathbf{v}, \mathbf{R}, \boldsymbol{\omega})$ and required motor thrust inputs $(u_1, u_2, u_3, u_4)$ can be computed algebraically without performing numerical ODE integration.
