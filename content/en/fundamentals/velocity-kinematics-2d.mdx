# Differential-Drive Velocity Kinematics

A differential-drive mobile robot controls motion by applying independent wheel speeds to left and right drive wheels separated by wheelbase $L$.

## Forward Kinematic Mapping

$$v = \frac{v_R + v_L}{2}, \quad \omega = \frac{v_R - v_L}{L}, \quad R_{ICC} = \frac{L}{2} \left(\frac{v_R + v_L}{v_R - v_L}\right)$$

### Non-Holonomic No-Slip Constraint
The wheels cannot slide laterally without rolling. In the robot frame, lateral velocity is strictly zero:

$$-\dot{x}\sin\theta + \dot{y}\cos\theta = 0$$
