# Dot Product, Orthogonality & Vector Projection (M02)

> "When a robot tracks a planned path or computes lateral steering error, how do we mathematically measure alignment and project one vector onto another?"

The **dot product** (also called the scalar product or inner product) is one of the most widely utilized mathematical operations in autonomous robotics. It serves as the foundation for **cross-track error calculation**, **Pure Pursuit steering**, **LiDAR surface normal estimation**, and **Iterative Closest Point (ICP)**.

---

## 1. Algebraic vs. Geometric Definitions

For two vectors $\mathbf{a} = [a_x, a_y]^T$ and $\mathbf{b} = [b_x, b_y]^T$ with angle $\theta$ between them:

### 1.1 Algebraic Definition (Component Sum)
$$
\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = a_x b_x + a_y b_y = \sum_{i=1}^n a_i b_i
$$

### 1.2 Geometric Definition (Cosine Formula)
$$
\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = \|\mathbf{a}\| \|\mathbf{b}\| \cos\theta
$$

Equating both definitions yields the angle $\theta$:

$$
\theta = \arccos\left( \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{a}\| \|\mathbf{b}\|} \right)
$$

---

## 2. Derivation: Vector Projection & Orthogonal Decomposition

In path tracking algorithms (such as Stanley or MPC), a robot needs to decompose its position displacement vector $\mathbf{a}$ into:
1. **Parallel component** $\mathbf{a}_\parallel$ along the path direction $\mathbf{b}$ (longitudinal progress).
2. **Perpendicular component** $\mathbf{a}_\perp$ orthogonal to $\mathbf{b}$ (**lateral cross-track error $e_{\text{lat}}$**).

```text
                  a
                 /|
                / |  a_perp (Cross-Track Error)
               /  |
              /___|____________ b (Path Direction)
                a_parallel (Projection)
```

### 2.1 Scalar Projection (Length of Shadow)
The signed scalar length of vector $\mathbf{a}$ projected onto the unit direction of $\mathbf{b}$ is:

$$
\text{comp}_{\mathbf{b}}(\mathbf{a}) = \|\mathbf{a}\| \cos\theta = \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{b}\|}
$$

### 2.2 Vector Projection
Multiplying the scalar length by unit vector $\hat{\mathbf{b}} = \frac{\mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{b}\|}$ gives the projection vector:

$$
\mathbf{a}_\parallel = \text{proj}_{\mathbf{b}}(\mathbf{a}) = \left( \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{b}\|^2} \right) \mathbf{b}
$$

### 2.3 Orthogonal Cross-Track Error Vector
$$
\mathbf{a}_\perp = \mathbf{a} - \mathbf{a}_\parallel = \mathbf{a} - \left( \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{b}\|^2} \right) \mathbf{b}
$$
