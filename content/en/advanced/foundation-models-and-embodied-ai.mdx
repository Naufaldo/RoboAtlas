# Foundation Models & Embodied AI: VLA, Diffusion Policy & World Models

> "What changes when a robot's 'brain' is not a hand-engineered state machine but a trillion-parameter Vision-Language-Action model pre-trained on the entire internet — and how does giving a robot a world model allow it to plan and imagine consequences before taking physical actions?"

The convergence of **large-scale deep learning** with **physical robotics** is producing a new paradigm: **Embodied AI**. Rather than learning task-specific controllers from scratch, modern robots leverage foundation models — massive neural networks pre-trained on internet-scale data (text, images, video, robot demonstrations) — to acquire **generalizable robotic intelligence**: language grounding, zero-shot task transfer, and abstract causal reasoning about physical consequences.

## 1. The Transformer Architecture in Robotics

All modern foundation models are built on the **Transformer** (Vaswani et al., 2017) — a neural network architecture based on **self-attention**:

$$\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) V$$

Where $Q$ (queries), $K$ (keys), and $V$ (values) are linear projections of the input sequence. The scaling factor $\frac{1}{\sqrt{d_k}}$ prevents gradient vanishing in high-dimensional key spaces.

Multi-Head Attention processes $h$ parallel attention heads:
$$\text{MultiHead}(Q, K, V) = \text{Concat}(\text{head}_1, \ldots, \text{head}_h) W^O$$
$$\text{head}_i = \text{Attention}(Q W_i^Q, K W_i^K, V W_i^V)$$

For robotics, **tokens** represent:
- **Image patches** (ViT-style visual tokenization)
- **Language instructions** ("Pick up the red cube")
- **Robot joint states and actions** $(\mathbf{q}, \dot{\mathbf{q}}, \tau)$

## 2. Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models

**VLA models** extend Vision-Language Models (VLMs like PaLM-E, Gemini) by adding an **action head** that directly outputs robot motor commands:

$$\underbrace{[\text{img}_t, \text{lang}, \mathbf{q}_t]}_{\text{Multimodal Inputs}} \xrightarrow{\text{VLA Transformer}} \underbrace{\mathbf{a}_{t:t+H}}_{\text{Action Chunk}}$$

Notable VLA systems:
- **RT-2** (Brohan et al., 2023): PaLM-E backbone with fine-tuned action tokens; zero-shot generalization to unseen objects
- **OpenVLA** (Kim et al., 2024): Open-source 7B parameter Llama-2 backbone with robot action fine-tuning
- **$\pi_0$ (Pi-Zero)** (Black et al., 2024): Flow matching generative VLA for dexterous bimanual manipulation

## 3. Diffusion Policy for Robot Control

**Diffusion Policy** (Chi et al., 2023) frames robot action generation as a **denoising diffusion process** — learning to reverse a Gaussian noise corruption chain:

**Forward process** (adding noise):
$$q(\mathbf{a}^k | \mathbf{a}^0) = \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{a}^k; \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_k} \mathbf{a}^0, (1 - \bar{\alpha}_k) I)$$

**Reverse process** (score matching denoising, conditioned on observation $\mathbf{o}$):
$$p_\theta(\mathbf{a}^{k-1} | \mathbf{a}^k, \mathbf{o}) = \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{a}^{k-1}; \boldsymbol{\mu}_\theta(\mathbf{a}^k, k, \mathbf{o}), \sigma_k^2 I)$$

Where the neural network $\epsilon_\theta$ predicts the noise:
$$\boldsymbol{\mu}_\theta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_k}} \left( \mathbf{a}^k - \frac{1 - \alpha_k}{\sqrt{1 - \bar{\alpha}_k}} \epsilon_\theta(\mathbf{a}^k, k, \mathbf{o}) \right)$$

**Key advantage**: Diffusion policy naturally represents **multi-modal action distributions** — a robot can learn to "either grasp from the left OR from the right" without mode averaging.

## 4. World Models: Dreaming Before Acting

**World Models** (Ha & Schmidhuber, 2018; DreamerV3, Hafner et al., 2023) enable robots to **imagine future consequences** of actions in a learned latent space, without executing them physically:

```text
  World Model Architecture:

  Observation o_t ──► [Encoder] ──► z_t (latent state)
                                      │
                          a_t ──────► [Transition Model] ──► z_{t+1} (imagined next state)
                                      │
                               ──────► [Reward Predictor] ──► r̂_t
                                      │
                               ──────► [Decoder] ──► ô_{t+1} (imagined observation)
```

The latent state dynamics follow:
$$h_t = f_\phi(h_{t-1}, z_{t-1}, a_{t-1}) \quad \text{(Recurrent State)}$$
$$z_t \sim q_\phi(z_t | h_t, o_t) \quad \text{(Posterior Encoder)}$$
$$\hat{z}_t \sim p_\phi(\hat{z}_t | h_t) \quad \text{(Prior / Imagination)}$$

DreamerV3 trains a policy entirely **inside the world model's imagination** — achieving superhuman performance on 150+ diverse tasks without domain-specific tuning.

## 5. Embodied AI: Zero-Shot Generalization

The key promise of Embodied AI is **zero-shot task transfer** — a robot trained on diverse demonstrations can generalize to completely novel tasks via language instruction:

```text
  Zero-Shot Generalization Pipeline:

  "Place the apple in the bowl"
        │
        ▼ (Language Grounding)
  [VLA Model] ── Visual perception: identify apple (novel color), bowl (novel shape)
        │
        ▼ (Causal Reasoning)
  Plan: grasp_apple → move_to_bowl → release
        │
        ▼ (Motor Execution)
  Joint torques τ_0, τ_1, ..., τ_T (60Hz)
```

This emerges from **scale**: RT-2's robot generalization abilities emerge from internet-scale pre-training on 540B tokens — the network learns physical intuitions from watching videos of humans cooking, cleaning, and assembling objects.
