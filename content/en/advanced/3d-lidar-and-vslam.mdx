# 3D LiDAR SLAM (LOAM/FAST-LIO) & Visual SLAM (VSLAM)

> "How do high-speed autonomous vehicles and aerial drones map the real world in full 6-DOF ($SE(3)$) using high-curvature 3D LiDAR point clouds and tightly-coupled visual-inertial camera streams?"

Three-dimensional autonomous robot navigation requires full 6-Degrees-of-Freedom state estimation ($\mathbf{x} = [x, y, z, \phi, \theta, \psi]^T \in SE(3)$). The two foremost paradigms powering modern spatial autonomy are **3D LiDAR SLAM** and **Visual SLAM (VSLAM)**.

![Comparison of 3D LiDAR SLAM and Visual SLAM](/images/sensors/3d-lidar-loam-fastlio-vslam.jpg)

## 1. 3D LiDAR SLAM: From LOAM to FAST-LIO2

### A. LOAM Geometric Curvature Feature Extraction
For point $\mathbf{p}_{(k,i)}$ in LiDAR scan frame $k$ with local neighborhood set $S$:

$$
c = \frac{1}{|S| \cdot \|\mathbf{p}_{(k,i)}\|} \left\| \sum_{j \in S, j \neq i} (\mathbf{p}_{(k,i)} - \mathbf{p}_{(k,j)}) \right\|
$$

- High curvature $c \to$ **Edge / Corner Features** $\to$ matched against nearest line segments.
- Low curvature $c \to$ **Planar / Flat Surface Features** $\to$ matched against nearest tangent planes.

### B. FAST-LIO2 & Incremental KD-Tree (`ikd-Tree`)
- Eliminates manual feature extraction overhead by matching raw laser points directly (*direct point-to-plane residual registration*).
- Uses an **`ikd-Tree` (Incremental KD-Tree)** data structure supporting dynamic point insertions, deletions, and $k$-NN searches in $O(\log N)$ time, enabling direct LiDAR odometry updates at rates exceeding $100\text{ Hz}$.

---

## 2. Visual SLAM (VSLAM): Epipolar Geometry & Triangulation

### A. Monocular Cameras & The Essential Matrix ($\mathbf{E}$)
Calibrated normalized image coordinates $\mathbf{x}_1, \mathbf{x}_2$ satisfy the fundamental epipolar constraint:

$$
\mathbf{x}_2^T \mathbf{E} \mathbf{x}_1 = 0, \quad \text{where } \mathbf{E} = [\mathbf{t}]_{\times} \mathbf{R}
$$

*(Monocular visual SLAM exhibits scale ambiguity; metric scale is unobservable without IMU pre-integration or known external fiducials).*

### B. Stereo Cameras & Disparity Triangulation
For a calibrated stereo rig with focal length $f$ and baseline distance $B$:

$$
Z = \frac{f \cdot B}{d}, \quad \text{where } d = u_L - u_R \text{ (pixel disparity)}
$$

### C. Tightly-Coupled Multi-Sensor Fusion (LVI-SAM & R3LIVE)
- **LVI-SAM**: Integrates a LiDAR-Inertial subsystem (LIS) and Visual-Inertial subsystem (VIS) into a joint factor graph with cross-system initialization and loop closure.
- **R3LIVE**: Fuses LiDAR and IMU measurements with an Iterated Error-State Kalman Filter (IESKF) at high frequency, using photometric bundle adjustment to colorize 3D point cloud maps with real-time RGB textures.
