# Formation Control & Leader-Follower Swarms (M26)

> "How do large-scale multi-rotor drone swarms and fleets of autonomous surface vessels (USVs) maintain rigid geometric formations (triangles, V-wedges, columns) during dynamic maneuvers while autonomously avoiding inter-robot collisions?"

In distributed multi-robot systems, **Formation Control** synthesizes algebraic graph theory, Leader-Follower tracking dynamics, and artificial potential repulsion fields to guarantee flocking stability.

---

## 1. Communication Network Topology & Graph Laplacian

The inter-agent communication topology is formalized via graph $\mathcal{G} = (\mathcal{V}, \mathcal{E})$:

$$
\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{D} - \mathbf{A}
$$

Where $\mathbf{D}$ is the in-degree diagonal matrix and $\mathbf{A}$ is the adjacency matrix. The existence of a directed spanning tree guarantees asymptotic geometric consensus.

---

## 2. Distributed Leader-Follower Control Law

Given leader trajectory $\mathbf{p}_0(t)$ and desired relative inter-agent offsets $\mathbf{d}_{ij} = \mathbf{p}_i^* - \mathbf{p}_j^*$, the acceleration control law for follower agent $i$ is:

$$
\mathbf{\dot{p}}_i = \mathbf{\dot{p}}_0 - k_p (\mathbf{p}_i - \mathbf{p}_0 - \mathbf{d}_{i0}) - k_v (\mathbf{v}_i - \mathbf{v}_0) - \sum_{j \in \mathcal{N}_i} a_{ij} (\mathbf{p}_i - \mathbf{p}_j - \mathbf{d}_{ij}) + \mathbf{F}_{\text{rep}, i}
$$

Where:
1. **Leader Feedforward Tracking**: Propagates global mission heading and velocity from leader $\mathbf{p}_0$.
2. **Relative Neighbor Consensus**: Minimizes geometric shape distortion across local neighbors $\mathcal{N}_i$.
3. **Inter-Agent Repulsion Field ($\mathbf{F}_{\text{rep}, i}$)**:
   $$\mathbf{F}_{\text{rep}, i} = \sum_{j \neq i, \, \|\mathbf{p}_i - \mathbf{p}_j\| < r_{\text{safe}}} k_{\text{rep}} \frac{\mathbf{p}_i - \mathbf{p}_j}{\|\mathbf{p}_i - \mathbf{p}_j\|} (r_{\text{safe}} - \|\mathbf{p}_i - \mathbf{p}_j\|)$$
