# Iterative Closest Point (ICP) Scan Registration in SLAM

> "How can a robot align two successive laser point clouds to accurately estimate its motion without relying on wheel encoders or GPS?"

In Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM), **Iterative Closest Point (ICP)** is the foundational algorithm for calculating the rigid transformation matrix $T = (R, \mathbf{t}) \in SE(2)$ that aligns a current sensor scan point set $P = \{\mathbf{p}_i\}$ onto a reference map point set $Q = \{\mathbf{q}_j\}$.

---

## 1. The ICP Least-Squares Objective

ICP seeks the rotation matrix $R \in SO(2)$ and translation vector $\mathbf{t}$ that minimize the sum of squared Euclidean correspondence errors:

$$
E(R, \mathbf{t}) = \sum_{i=1}^N \| \mathbf{q}_i - (R \mathbf{p}_i + \mathbf{t}) \|^2
$$

---

## 2. Closed-Form SVD Solution (Per Iteration)

### Step 1: Compute Centroids
Compute center-of-mass centroids for both point sets:

$$
\boldsymbol{\mu}_p = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \mathbf{p}_i, \quad \boldsymbol{\mu}_q = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \mathbf{q}_i
$$

### Step 2: Demeaned Points & Cross-Covariance Matrix $H$
Subtract centroids $\mathbf{p}'_i = \mathbf{p}_i - \boldsymbol{\mu}_p$ and $\mathbf{q}'_i = \mathbf{q}_i - \boldsymbol{\mu}_q$:

$$
H = \sum_{i=1}^N \mathbf{p}'_i (\mathbf{q}'_i)^T
$$

### Step 3: Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)
Decompose cross-covariance matrix $H$:

$$
H = U \Sigma V^T
$$

The optimal orthogonal rotation matrix $R$ is given by:

$$
R = V U^T
$$

*(If $\det(R) = -1$, correct the reflection: $R = V \operatorname{diag}(1, \dots, \det(VU^T)) U^T$)*.

### Step 4: Optimal Translation Vector $\mathbf{t}$
$$
\mathbf{t} = \boldsymbol{\mu}_q - R \boldsymbol{\mu}_p
$$
