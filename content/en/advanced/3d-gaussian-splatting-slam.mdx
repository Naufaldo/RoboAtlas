# 3D Gaussian Splatting SLAM (3DGS-SLAM) & Radiance Fields

> "Why are traditional Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) computationally bottlenecked by millions of neural network evaluations per frame, while 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) renders photorealistic volumetric scenes at 60+ FPS while simultaneously tracking 6-DOF camera poses?"

**3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS)** has revolutionized Spatial AI and robotic scene representation. Unlike implicit continuous neural representations that require dense raymarching with thousands of MLP queries per pixel, 3DGS parameterizes physical environments as millions of **Explicit Anisotropic 3D Gaussian Ellipsoids**.

---

## 1. Mathematical Formulation of 3D Gaussian Primitives

Each 3D Gaussian primitive $G(\mathbf{x})$ located in Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^3$ evaluates to:

$$
G(\mathbf{x}) = \exp\left( -\frac{1}{2} (\mathbf{x} - \boldsymbol{\mu})^T \mathbf{\Sigma}^{-1} (\mathbf{x} - \boldsymbol{\mu}) \right)
$$

To enforce positive semi-definiteness on the covariance matrix $\mathbf{\Sigma} \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3}$, $\mathbf{\Sigma}$ is parameterized via rotation quaternion matrix $\mathbf{R}$ and scaling diagonal matrix $\mathbf{S} = \text{diag}(s_x, s_y, s_z)$:

$$
\mathbf{\Sigma} = \mathbf{R} \mathbf{S} \mathbf{S}^T \mathbf{R}^T
$$

Each Gaussian additionally stores an opacity $\alpha \in [0, 1]$ and view-dependent radiance colors modeled through **Spherical Harmonics (SH)** coefficients.

---

## 2. 2D Projective Splatting (EWA) & Differentiable Tile Rasterization

When observed by a camera with world-to-camera matrix $\mathbf{W}$ and pinhole Jacobian $\mathbf{J}$, the 2D projected screen covariance $\mathbf{\Sigma}' \in \mathbb{R}^{2 \times 2}$ is computed in closed form:

$$
\mathbf{\Sigma}' = \mathbf{J} \mathbf{W} \mathbf{\Sigma} \mathbf{W}^T \mathbf{J}^T
$$

### Opacity-Weighted Front-to-Back Alpha Blending:

Pixel color $C$ is computed across sorted overlapping Gaussians:

$$
C = \sum_{i \in \mathcal{N}} c_i \alpha_i \prod_{j=1}^{i-1} (1 - \alpha_j)
$$

---

## 3. Real-Time Camera Tracking & Dynamic Densification

3DGS-SLAM orchestrates two concurrent GPU threads:
1. **Camera Pose Tracking**: Minimizes photometric rendering loss against incoming camera frames:
   $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{tracking}} = (1 - \lambda) \|I - \hat{I}\|_1 + \lambda \, \text{D-SSIM}(I, \hat{I})$$
2. **Adaptive Densification & Pruning**: Splits high-gradient Gaussians in under-reconstructed areas and purges transparent primitives ($\alpha < 0.05$).
