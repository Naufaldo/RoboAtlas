# Multi-Sensor Fusion: Wheel Odometry + IMU + GPS (M30)

> "Why is no single sensor sufficient on its own (wheel odometry drifts from kinematic wheel slip, IMU gyro drifts from bias random walk, and GPS exhibits erratic Gaussian multipath noise)—and how does multi-sensor fusion synthesize them into an accurate, drift-free pose estimate?"

In outdoor autonomous mobile robotics (agricultural rovers, self-driving vehicles, search-and-rescue UAVs), navigation reliability depends on **Heterogeneous Multi-Sensor Fusion**.

## 2. Mathematical Multi-Rate Kalman Fusion Architecture

The augmented state vector models robot position and linear velocity $\mathbf{x}_k = [x, y, v_x, v_y]^T$:

### 1. High-Rate Prediction Phase (Driven by Odometry & IMU):

$$
\mathbf{\hat{x}}_k^- = \mathbf{F}_k \mathbf{\hat{x}}_{k-1} + \mathbf{B}_k \mathbf{u}_k
$$

$$
\mathbf{P}_k^- = \mathbf{F}_k \mathbf{P}_{k-1} \mathbf{F}_k^T + \mathbf{Q}_k
$$

### 2. Low-Rate Absolute Correction Phase (When GPS Arrives):

$$
\mathbf{K}_k = \mathbf{P}_k^- \mathbf{H}_k^T (\mathbf{H}_k \mathbf{P}_k^- \mathbf{H}_k^T + \mathbf{R}_k)^{-1}
$$

$$
\mathbf{\hat{x}}_k = \mathbf{\hat{x}}_k^- + \mathbf{K}_k (\mathbf{z}_{\text{GPS}, k} - \mathbf{H}_k \mathbf{\hat{x}}_k^-)
$$

$$
\mathbf{P}_k = (\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{K}_k \mathbf{H}_k) \mathbf{P}_k^-
$$

Where optimal Kalman gain $\mathbf{K}_k$ dynamically balances internal motion uncertainty $\mathbf{P}_k^-$ against noisy GPS measurement covariance $\mathbf{R}_k$.
