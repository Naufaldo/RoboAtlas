# Histogram Filter / Discrete Grid-Based Topological Localization

> "What happens when a robot's spatial belief has multiple distinct peaks (multi-modal) that cannot be approximated by a single bell-shaped Gaussian distribution?"

Kalman filters are structurally constrained by their unimodal Gaussian assumption. However, in **Global Localization**, a robot observing a door in a symmetric hallway with three identical doors must maintain **three distinct positional hypotheses simultaneously**. The **Histogram Filter (Discrete Grid-Based Bayes Filter)** quantizes continuous state space into discrete bins $\{x_1, x_2, \dots, x_N\}$ and maintains exact probability masses across all bins without parametric Gaussian restrictions.

## 1. Mathematical Formulation: Discrete Bayesian Filter

The state space is partitioned into $N$ mutually exclusive cells. The belief vector is represented as:

$$
\mathbf{p}_t = [p_t(x_1), p_t(x_2), \dots, p_t(x_N)]^T, \quad \text{where } \sum_{i=1}^N p_t(x_i) = 1.0
$$

### Step 1: Motion Prediction (Discrete State Transition Convolution)
When the robot executes action command $u_t$, the transition probability of moving from cell $x_j$ to cell $x_i$ is governed by $P(x_i \mid u_t, x_j)$:

$$
\bar{p}_t(x_i) = \sum_{j=1}^N P(x_i \mid u_t, x_j) \, p_{t-1}(x_j)
$$

*(Actuator motion uncertainty diffuses probability mass across neighboring grid bins).*

### Step 2: Measurement Correction (Likelihood Multiplication & Normalization)
When exteroceptive measurement $z_t$ is recorded with sensor likelihood model $P(z_t \mid x_i)$:

$$
p_t(x_i) = \eta \cdot P(z_t \mid x_i) \cdot \bar{p}_t(x_i)
$$

Where normalization factor $\eta$ guarantees that total probability sums to unity:

$$
\eta = \frac{1}{\sum_{k=1}^N P(z_t \mid x_k) \bar{p}_t(x_k)}
$$

---

## 2. Advantages of Non-Parametric Multi-Modal Beliefs

1. **True Multi-Modality**: Naturally represents arbitrary, asymmetric, or multi-peaked probability distributions (e.g., symmetric repetitive buildings).
2. **Zero Linearization Required**: Does not require differentiability, Taylor series, or Jacobian matrices.
3. **Natural Disambiguation**: As the robot drives and collects sequential observations, false hypotheses are exponentially suppressed, collapsing the multi-modal belief into a sharp single peak (*true pose*).
