# Oriented Rectangle (OBB) & L-Shape Bounding Box Fitting

> "Once raw laser returns are clustered into an obstacle object, how does an autonomous vehicle accurately estimate the obstacle's length, width, center coordinate, and heading angle?"

In autonomous driving, a 2D LiDAR scanner typically observes only the two nearest perpendicular faces of another vehicle, creating a characteristic **L-Shape point cloud pattern**. The **Oriented Bounding Box (OBB) / L-Shape Fitting** algorithm searches for the optimal orientation angle $\psi^* \in [0^\circ, 90^\circ]$ to tightly enclose the point cluster with a minimal oriented bounding rectangle.

---

## 1. Optimal Search Angle Optimization

Given a segmented obstacle point cluster $\mathcal{P} = \{(x_i, y_i)\}_{i=1}^N$:

### Stage 1: Rotate Points into Trial Orientation $\psi$
For each trial heading angle $\psi \in [0^\circ, 90^\circ)$ with angular search step $\Delta \psi$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} x_i' \\ y_i' \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos(-\psi) & -\sin(-\psi) \\ \sin(-\psi) & \cos(-\psi) \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x_i \\ y_i \end{bmatrix}
$$

### Stage 2: Compute Extrema Dimensions
Calculate bounding spans in the rotated coordinate frame:

$$
L(\psi) = \max_{i} x_i' - \min_{i} x_i', \quad W(\psi) = \max_{i} y_i' - \min_{i} y_i'
$$

### Stage 3: Evaluate Fitting Cost Criteria

1. **Minimum Area Criterion**:
   $$
   \psi^* = \arg\min_{\psi} \left( L(\psi) \cdot W(\psi) \right)
   $$
2. **Variance Criterion (Edge Compactness)**:
   Minimizes residual variance of points along the two principal orthogonal bounding edges:
   $$
   \sigma^2(\psi) = \frac{1}{N-1} \sum_{i=1}^N \min\left( (x_i' - \bar{x}')^2, (y_i' - \bar{y}')^2 \right)
   $$

---

## 2. Obstacle State Tuple Output

Once the optimal heading $\psi^*$ is found, the obstacle centroid $(x_c, y_c)$ in the global world frame is computed by rotating the local bounding center back to world coordinates:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} x_c \\ y_c \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{R}(\psi^*) \begin{bmatrix} \frac{x_{\min}' + x_{\max}'}{2} \\ \frac{y_{\min}' + y_{\max}'}{2} \end{bmatrix}
$$

Output: A complete physical obstacle state tuple $(x_c, y_c, \psi^*, L, W)$ ready for downstream Extended Kalman Filter object tracking and dynamic overtaking trajectory planners.
