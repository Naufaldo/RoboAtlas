# Log-Odds Bayesian Occupancy Grid Mapping

> "How does a robot convert noisy 2D laser range scans into a crisp, permanent binary map of free and occupied space?"

Occupancy grid mapping represents the environment as a 2D tessellation of independent binary random variables $m_i \in \{0, 1\}$ (0 = Free space, 1 = Occupied obstacle).

![2D Occupancy Grid Mapping with Log-Odds](/images/sensors/occupancy-grid-logodds.jpg)

---

## 1. The Log-Odds Representation

Standard probability multiplication $p(m_i \mid z_{1:t})$ suffers from numerical floating-point underflow. By converting probabilities into **Log-Odds** form:

$$
l_t(m_i) = \ln\left( \frac{p(m_i \mid z_{1:t})}{1 - p(m_i \mid z_{1:t})} \right)
$$

### Additive Update Rule:
The recursive Bayesian update becomes a fast, numerically stable simple addition:

$$
l_t(m_i) = l_{t-1}(m_i) + \text{inverse\_sensor\_model}(m_i, x_t, z_t) - l_0
$$

where $l_0 = \ln\frac{p_0}{1 - p_0}$ is the prior occupancy belief (typically $p_0 = 0.5 \implies l_0 = 0$).

---

## 2. The Inverse Sensor Model for LiDAR Raycasting

When a laser beam travels distance $z_t$ along angle $\theta$:

1. **Free Space Cells ($r < z_t - \frac{\text{thickness}}{2}$)**:
   - Decrease log-odds by $l_{\text{free}} = \ln\frac{0.3}{0.7} \approx -0.847$.
2. **Occupied Obstacle Cells ($|r - z_t| \le \frac{\text{thickness}}{2}$)**:
   - Increase log-odds by $l_{\text{occ}} = \ln\frac{0.85}{0.15} \approx +1.734$.
3. **Unobserved Cells ($r > z_t + \frac{\text{thickness}}{2}$)**:
   - Unchanged ($\Delta l = 0$).

To recover true probability from log-odds:

$$
p(m_i) = 1 - \frac{1}{1 + \exp(l(m_i))}
$$
