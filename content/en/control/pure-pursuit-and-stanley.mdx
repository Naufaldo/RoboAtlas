# Path Tracking Feedback Control

Path tracking controllers compute the steering angle $\delta(t)$ required to guide a vehicle along a reference geometric path.

## Pure Pursuit Geometric Steering

$$\delta = \arctan\left( \frac{2 L \sin\alpha}{L_f} \right)$$

## Stanley Front Axle Non-Linear Control

$$\delta(t) = \theta_e(t) + \arctan\left( \frac{k \cdot e(t)}{v(t)} \right)$$
