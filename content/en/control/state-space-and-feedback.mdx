# State-Space Representation, Phase Portraits & Full-State Feedback (M27)

> "How do modern aerospace autopilots, robotic manipulators, and active suspension systems control complex multi-variable systems with multiple inputs and outputs simultaneously?"

**State-Space Modeling** reformulates high-order differential equations into a compact first-order vector matrix differential system:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dot{\mathbf{x}}(t) &= A\mathbf{x}(t) + B\mathbf{u}(t) \\
\mathbf{y}(t) &= C\mathbf{x}(t) + D\mathbf{u}(t)
\end{aligned}
$$

---

## 1. Converting Physical Systems to State-Space: The Mass-Spring-Damper

Consider a mechanical system of mass $m$, spring constant $k$, and damping coefficient $c$ driven by actuator force $u(t)$:

$$
m \ddot{x} + c \dot{x} + k x = u(t)
$$

### 1.1 State Vector Definition
Define state variables: $x_1 = x$ (position) and $x_2 = \dot{x}$ (velocity):

$$
\mathbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} x \\ \dot{x} \end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 State Equations
Taking time derivatives:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dot{x}_1 &= x_2 \\
\dot{x}_2 &= \ddot{x} = -\frac{k}{m}x_1 - \frac{c}{m}x_2 + \frac{1}{m}u
\end{aligned}
$$

In matrix form $\dot{\mathbf{x}} = A\mathbf{x} + B\mathbf{u}$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} \dot{x}_1 \\ \dot{x}_2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -\frac{k}{m} & -\frac{c}{m} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ \frac{1}{m} \end{bmatrix} u
$$

---

## 2. Full-State Feedback Control: $u = -K\mathbf{x}$

By measuring all state variables ($x, \dot{x}$), we apply linear state feedback:

$$
u = -K\mathbf{x} = -\begin{bmatrix} K_1 & K_2 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \end{bmatrix} = -K_1 x - K_2 \dot{x}
$$

Substituting $u$ into the system dynamics yields the **closed-loop state equation**:

$$
\dot{\mathbf{x}} = (A - BK)\mathbf{x}
$$

The closed-loop eigenvalues (poles) $\det(sI - (A - BK)) = 0$ can be placed anywhere in the stable left-half complex plane to achieve desired settling times and damping ratios.
