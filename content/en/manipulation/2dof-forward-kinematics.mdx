# 2-DOF Planar Arm Forward Kinematics & Workspace Sweep (M18)

> "Given the motor joint angles (q1, q2) of a robotic arm, where will its end-effector tool tip land in Cartesian workspace coordinates?"

**Forward Kinematics (FK)** maps joint space variables $\mathbf{q} \in \mathbb{R}^n$ to operational Cartesian space coordinates $\mathbf{x} \in \text{SE}(2)$ or $\text{SE}(3)$.

---

## 1. Geometric Derivation of the 2-Link Serial Chain

Consider a 2-degree-of-freedom planar robot arm with link lengths $L_1, L_2$ and joint angles $q_1, q_2$:

```text
       Joint 2 (Elbow)
          o────────────o End-Effector (x_E, y_E)
         /   q2         Link 2 (L2)
        /
       / Link 1 (L1)
      /
     o Base (0, 0)
     q1
```

### 1.1 Joint 1 Position (Elbow Joint)
$$
\begin{aligned}
x_1 &= L_1 \cos(q_1) \\
y_1 &= L_1 \sin(q_1)
\end{aligned}
$$

### 1.2 Joint 2 Position (End-Effector Tip)
Adding the second link's projection at relative angle $(q_1 + q_2)$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_E &= L_1 \cos(q_1) + L_2 \cos(q_1 + q_2) \\
y_E &= L_1 \sin(q_1) + L_2 \sin(q_1 + q_2) \\
\phi_E &= q_1 + q_2 \quad (\text{End-effector orientation})
\end{aligned}
$$

---

## 2. Workspace Boundaries & Annulus Geometry

The **Reachable Workspace** $\mathcal{W}$ is the set of all Cartesian points $(x, y)$ reachable by the end-effector:

1. **Maximum Reach ($R_{\max}$)**: Occurs when $q_2 = 0^\circ$ (arm fully outstretched):
   $$
   R_{\max} = L_1 + L_2
   $$
2. **Minimum Reach ($R_{\min}$)**: Occurs when $q_2 = 180^\circ$ (arm folded back on itself):
   $$
   R_{\min} = |L_1 - L_2|
   $$
3. **Workspace Area**:
   $$
   \text{Area} = \pi \left( R_{\max}^2 - R_{\min}^2 \right) = \pi \left( (L_1 + L_2)^2 - (L_1 - L_2)^2 \right) = 4\pi L_1 L_2
   $$

*(Notice that workspace area is maximized when $L_1 = L_2$).*
