# 2-DOF Planar Arm Analytical Inverse Kinematics (M19)

> "In Forward Kinematics, motor angles determine the tool tip position. In Inverse Kinematics, we solve the reverse: Given an obstacle or object coordinate (x, y), what motor angles (q1, q2) must the robot actuate?"

**Inverse Kinematics (IK)** is fundamental for pick-and-place manipulation, trajectory execution, and robotic welding.

---

## 1. Geometric Derivation via the Law of Cosines

Consider a 2-link planar arm with link lengths $L_1, L_2$ trying to reach target position $(x, y)$:

```text
               Target (x, y)
                     o
                    / \
                   /   \
              L2  /     \
                 /  q2   \  r = sqrt(x^2 + y^2)
     Elbow Joint o        \
                /          \
            L1 /     psi    \
              /              \
             o────────────────o Base (0, 0)
            beta
```

### 1.1 Distance to Target ($r$)
$$
r^2 = x^2 + y^2
$$

### 1.2 Elbow Angle ($q_2$) Derivation
Applying the Law of Cosines to the triangle formed by the base, elbow, and target:

$$
r^2 = L_1^2 + L_2^2 - 2 L_1 L_2 \cos(180^\circ - q_2) = L_1^2 + L_2^2 + 2 L_1 L_2 \cos(q_2)
$$

Solving for $\cos(q_2)$:

$$
\cos(q_2) = \frac{x^2 + y^2 - L_1^2 - L_2^2}{2 L_1 L_2}
$$

Because $\sin(q_2) = \pm \sqrt{1 - \cos^2(q_2)}$, there are **two distinct valid branch solutions**:

$$
q_2 = \text{atan2}\left(\pm \sqrt{1 - \cos^2(q_2)}, \, \cos(q_2)\right)
$$

- **$+q_2$**: **Elbow-Up** branch configuration.
- **$-q_2$**: **Elbow-Down** branch configuration.

### 1.3 Shoulder Base Angle ($q_1$) Derivation
Decomposing the angle to the target $\beta = \text{atan2}(y, x)$ and the internal angle $\psi$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta &= \text{atan2}(y, x) \\
\psi &= \text{atan2}(L_2 \sin q_2, \, L_1 + L_2 \cos q_2) \\
q_1 &= \beta - \psi
\end{aligned}
$$

---

## 2. Workspace Reachability & Singularities

A closed-form solution exists if and only if the argument to $\arccos$ is within $[-1, +1]$:

$$
|L_1 - L_2| \le \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \le L_1 + L_2
$$

- **Out-of-Reach ($r > L_1 + L_2$)**: The target is outside the maximum reach of the arm.
- **Inner Hole ($r < |L_1 - L_2|$)**: The target is too close to the base for non-equal link lengths.
- **Kinematic Singularity ($q_2 = 0^\circ$ or $180^\circ$)**: The two branch solutions merge into a single degenerate configuration where instantaneous radial velocities cannot be generated.
